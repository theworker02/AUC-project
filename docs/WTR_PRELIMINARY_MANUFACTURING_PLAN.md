# WTR-GA-001 / WTR-FL-001 — AUC-Water AWG Preliminary Manufacturing Plan

**Issue:** P0 pilot-package quotation and design-review release only.  
**Boundary:** treated utility water. Potable and PEM-feed packages require separate approved treatment designs.

## 1. Four-bay arrangement

```text
AIR IN → [Bay 2: filter + moisture capture] → exhaust air
                   │
                   ▼
[Bay 1: power/HVAC] [Bay 3: covered sump/storage] → [Bay 4: treatment + delivery] → utility-water export
                                                           └→ separate qualified DI interface → Phyto-Breeze
```

| Bay | Fabrication content | Required design input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Power/HVAC | Compressor package, controls, electrical segregation, leak containment | Electrical load, refrigerant design, local code |
| 2 — Air extraction | Mechanical intake filter, fans, DX coil or selected contactor, sloped pan | Climate duty, air quality, cleaning access |
| 3 — Condensate/storage | Covered drainable sump, level instruments, overflow/cleanout | Tank volume, turnover, hygienic design |
| 4 — Treatment/delivery | Declared treatment barriers, sample ports, pump, backflow, reject routing | End-use water specification |

## 2. Required fabrication details

- Provide accessible coil and pan cleaning without entering another bay.
- Slope all condensate surfaces to a drain; avoid blind legs and stagnant reservoirs.
- Specify all wetted materials against the declared water chemistry and cleaning chemicals.
- Use mechanical filtration for coil protection. The baseline has no electrostatic precharger.
- Provide sampling points at raw condensate, post-treatment, tank outlet, and any Phyto-Breeze transfer boundary.
- Route RO/DI concentrate, cleaning fluids, overflow, and off-spec water to approved disposal paths; do not leave this as a field decision.
- Provide labelled non-potable piping and backflow protection; no cross-connection with potable plumbing.

## 3. Rating, FAT, and pilot acceptance

The nameplate must state temperature, RH, airflow, measurement duration, delivered-water boundary, L/h, and kWh/L. FAT verifies mechanical operation; field pilot data establishes production yield.

| Test | Minimum record |
| --- | --- |
| Air module | flow, pressure drop, entering/leaving state, condensate rate |
| Power | compressor/fan/treatment electrical energy by interval |
| Water quality | raw and delivered sample results against declared use |
| Storage | level, turnover, overflow, cleanout and off-spec diversion |
| Treatment | pressure, conductivity where applicable, reject volume, alarms |

## 4. Release blockers

Site psychrometrics, end-use water specification, treatment P&ID, refrigerant selection and safety design, drainage/reject-disposal plan, tank design, and vendor energy model are required before fabrication release.
