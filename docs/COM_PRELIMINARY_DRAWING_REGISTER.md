## Drawing conventions

- All dimensions are millimetres unless noted otherwise.
- `TBD` means no supplier may select or fabricate without approved engineering data.
- `HOLD` is an explicit stop point. It cannot be replaced by a vendor assumption.
- Process-media colors are informational only: air = blue, water = green, oxygen = yellow, hydrogen = red.
- No drawing authorizes the sharing of air, water, oxygen, hydrogen, relief, drain, or emergency-shutdown systems between portfolio modules.
