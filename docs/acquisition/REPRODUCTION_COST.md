## Why acquisition beats greenfield

Reproducing `AUC-project` means rediscovering domain constraints, re-implementing architecture and edge cases, rebuilding tests/CI/packaging, and rewriting diligence materials already present in this repository. Buying the transferable IP is the faster path to a production-ready starting point.

## Product thesis

AUC is a portfolio of independent environmental systems supported by shared evidence, telemetry, and energy planning. It is not a single combined hazardous-process machine.
