# AUC Verification and Acceptance Plan

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Calibrated direct measurement at the declared operating point.
2. Repeatable test using an approved method and traceable controls.
3. CFD or simulation correlated against physical data.
4. Vendor datasheet only for initial selection; never for final performance acceptance.

## AUC-Air acceptance records

| Test | Minimum record | Acceptance basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Airflow distribution | 25-point face-velocity map, flow calibration, temperature/RH | CV and local limits stated in the released design basis |
| Pressure drop | Stage curves at 50/75/100/110/125% flow, clean and loaded media | Released fan-system operating envelope |
| Leakage/bypass | Tracer or calibrated leakage test | Internal bypass limit in released drawing |
| PM capture | Paired upstream/downstream size-resolved data, corona OFF then ON | Predeclared statistical endpoint family |
| Gas conversion | Paired inlet/outlet species, RH, irradiance, residence time, byproducts | Predeclared penetration and byproduct bounds |
| Ozone control | Worst-case corona test plus MnO2 outlet measurement | Absolute outlet safety limit |
| Coating retention | Flow, start/stop, RH-cycle, particle, Ti/Mn results | Declared detection limits |
| Safety | Guard, airflow, fan-stall, overtemperature, sensor-failure, power-loss fault records | Cause-and-effect matrix |

## AUC-Water acceptance records

Measure gross condensate, treatment losses, delivered-water volume, power, ambient psychrometrics, water quality, tank turnover, and off-spec diversion. Report all values by interval rather than a single daily average.

## Phyto-Breeze acceptance records

The 25 kg/3 h product claim is evaluated at the post-conditioning delivery boundary using calibrated mass-flow, purity, temperature, pressure, moisture, crossover, and timing instrumentation. The lower one-sided 95% result must meet the declared product metric.
