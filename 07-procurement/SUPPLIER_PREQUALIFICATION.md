# Supplier Prequalification

| Category | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Capability | Comparable equipment, qualified personnel, fabrication scope, test capability |
| Quality | Quality system, traceability, calibration, NCR/corrective-action method |
| Safety | Applicable safety records, training, material/SDS controls |
| Capacity | Lead time, continuity, spare parts, service coverage |
| Commercial | Warranty, insurance where required, transparent quote exclusions |

Suppliers must disclose deviations. A quotation that silently replaces a released requirement is non-responsive.
