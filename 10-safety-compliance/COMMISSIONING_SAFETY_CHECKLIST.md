# Commissioning Safety Checklist

- Approved IFC package and site permits available.
- Mechanical completion, pressure/leak tests, electrical inspection, grounding, and labeling complete.
- Cause/effect testing complete with documented safe-state response.
- Instruments calibrated; alarms and interlocks function-tested.
- Relief, drainage, ventilation, access, LOTO, and emergency response verified.
- Operators trained; manuals, spares, and maintenance schedules handed over.
- No hazardous-process startup until responsible engineering and site authorities sign the commissioning release.
