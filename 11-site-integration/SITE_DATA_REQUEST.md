# Site Data Request

Collect before final equipment selection:

- Location, elevation, temperature, RH, wet-bulb, wind, rainfall, and particulate history.
- Electrical service voltage, available kW, demand constraints, grounding, and backup power.
- Water source, chemistry, drainage, sewer/disposal capacity, and end-use demand.
- Structural loading, foundation, wind/seismic exposure, access, crane path, and maintenance clearance.
- Applicable permitting, zoning, noise, environmental, fire, and hazardous-area requirements.
