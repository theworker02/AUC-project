Portfolio modules

AIR

AUC-Air

Fine-particle capture and defined gas-phase treatment with measured byproduct control.

  • Cyclone: coarse-load management only
  • PAN: fine PM performance owner
  • UVA PCO: requires independent qualification
WATER

AUC-Water

Lowest-energy source selection: condensate and rain first; AWG only after climate and pilot evidence.

  • Non-potable utility water is the initial boundary
  • Yield stated with climate and energy data
  • PEM water requires separate OEM-qualified DI treatment
O₂

Phyto-Breeze

Standalone PEM oxygen-production package with physically isolated hydrogen and oxygen process boundaries.

  • ≥25 kg verified delivered O₂ / 3 h target
  • H₂ and O₂ systems never share headers
  • Construction requires OEM and site safety package

Release control

What a manufacturer receives—and what remains open

Required before procurement

  1. Approved GA drawings and 3D model
  2. P&ID, line list, I/O and cause/effect matrix
  3. Electrical one-line, load, grounding, and cable schedules
  4. Approved vendor datasheets, inspection plan, FAT and SAT
  5. Site permits and applicable process-safety approvals

Current hold points

  1. AUC-Air: qualification results and final vendor data
  2. AUC-Water: site climate, water-quality target, treatment P&ID
  3. Phyto-Breeze: OEM P&ID/ICD, HAZOP/LOPA, area classification, relief and dispersion design
